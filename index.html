A simple rhythm for moving from “what if?” to “it works.”

01 — NOTICE

Start with a signal

Pay attention to the small friction points. Good work often begins with a quiet observation.

02 — SHAPE

Make it tangible

Give the idea a shape people can touch, test, and talk about before it gets too precious.

03 — GROW

Release with care

Learn from the real world, keep what helps, and let each version become a little more useful.
